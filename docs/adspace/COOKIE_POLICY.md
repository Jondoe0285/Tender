# Cookie Policy - Trade Tender Platform

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our application. They may contain information about your browsing activity, preferences, or login status.

Trade Tender uses cookies to:

- Maintain your login session and security
- Protect the platform from misuse

Trade Tender currently uses essential cookies only. Optional analytics, advertising, personalisation, and email-tracking technologies are not deployed. No optional tracker will be enabled until consent controls and the associated policy update are live.

## 2. Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are required for the platform to function correctly. They are deployed automatically and do not require your consent. Their lifetime is limited to what is necessary for secure session and platform operation.

**You cannot opt-out of essential cookies without affecting platform functionality.**

## 3. Optional Cookies (Not Active)

Trade Tender does not currently use analytics, advertising, personalisation, or third-party tracking cookies. The examples below are not deployed and are not a list of current platform cookies.

## 4. Advertising Cookies

Advertising cookies, targeted advertising, advertising profiles, and third-party advertising sharing are not active. They will remain inactive until an approved implementation provides consent controls and an updated policy before collection starts.

## 5. Your Cookie Choices

### 5.1 Cookie Consent Banner

There is no cookie preference centre at present because optional tracking is not active. Essential session and security cookies cannot be disabled without preventing secure operation.

### 5.2 Manage Your Preferences

Before optional technologies are introduced, Trade Tender will provide clear notice and appropriate consent controls.

### 5.3 Browser-Based Cookie Control

Your browser may allow you to:

- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete existing cookies
- View stored cookies

**Note**: Blocking cookies may prevent the platform from functioning correctly.

## 6. Cookie Retention and Deletion

Essential cookie lifetime is limited to what is necessary for secure session and platform operation. Trade Tender does not currently retain analytics, advertising, or cookie-consent records because those optional technologies are not active.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Trade Tender does not currently operate optional third-party tracking cookies.

### 7.1 Stripe (Payments)

- **Service**: Payment processing
- **Cookies**: Session and security cookies only
- **Privacy Policy**: [Stripe Privacy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy)

### 7.2 Email Service Provider

- **Service**: Email communication
- **Cookies**: No email-open or link-click tracking is enabled by Trade Tender

## 8. GDPR and Privacy Compliance

### 8.1 Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request all data held about you
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request data deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Restriction**: Limit how your data is processed
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection**: Object to specific processing activities

### 8.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

Authenticated users can submit an access/export, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection request through the platform Support requests area. Requests are assigned a 30-day due date and may be affected by legal retention, investigation, or legal-hold obligations.

## 9. Adspace-Specific Cookie Terms

- No third-party advertising data is shared

**When Adspace IS Active**:
- Advertising remains inactive until an approved implementation supplies consent controls
- No advertising cookies or targeted advertising are deployed before that implementation is live

## 10. Changes to This Policy
