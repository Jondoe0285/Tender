# Cookie Policy - Trade Tender Platform

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our application. They may contain information about your browsing activity, preferences, or login status.

Trade Tender uses cookies to:

- Maintain your login session and security
- Remember your preferences
- Analyze how you use our platform
- Display relevant advertising (when adspace is activated)
- Track advertising performance

## 2. Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are required for the platform to function correctly. They are deployed automatically and do not require your consent.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| `sessionId` | User session management and authentication | Session |
| `_csrf` | Cross-site request forgery protection | Session |
| `language` | Language preference | 1 year |
| `theme` | Visual theme preference (light/dark) | 1 year |

**You cannot opt-out of essential cookies without affecting platform functionality.**

## 3. Analytics Cookies (Requires Consent)

These cookies help Trade Tender understand how users interact with the platform. They collect anonymized usage information.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Provider | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics user identification | Google | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics session identification | Google | 24 hours |
| `_gat` | Google Analytics rate limiting | Google | 1 minute |

**Effect of Declining**: Trade Tender cannot track platform usage or identify problems affecting users.

## 4. Advertising Cookies (Requires Explicit Consent - Only When Adspace is Active)

**These cookies are only deployed when Trade Tender's adspace feature is activated.**

Advertising cookies allow Trade Tender and its advertising partners to:

- Display targeted advertising relevant to your interests
- Track whether you interact with advertising
- Measure advertising campaign performance
- Build audience profiles for Advertisers

### 4.1 Trade Tender Advertising Cookies

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| `adspace_consent` | Records your advertising cookie consent choice | 2 years |
| `adspace_tracking` | Tracks your interactions with Trade Tender ads | 6 months |
| `adspace_user_id` | Links your browsing to your Trade Tender account (anonymized) | 6 months |

### 4.2 Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Trade Tender may share information with external advertising platforms:

| Partner | Cookie Purpose | Data Shared | Opt-Out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads | Display targeted ads | Anonymized user ID, interests | [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) |
| Facebook Pixel | Retargeting on Facebook | Anonymized user activity | [Facebook Ad Preferences](https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences) |
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B advertising targeting | Anonymized professional profile | [LinkedIn Ad Preferences](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/62931) |

**Data Shared with Third Parties**: 
- Anonymized user identifiers
- Aggregated platform usage patterns
- Non-identifying campaign interaction data
- **NOT SHARED**: Email, phone number, sensitive business data, confidential bid information

## 5. Your Cookie Choices

### 5.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit Trade Tender or when adspace is activated, you will see a cookie consent banner offering options:

1. **Accept All**: Accepts all cookies including advertising cookies
2. **Essential Only**: Declines non-essential cookies (no tracking, no ads)
3. **Customize**: Lets you choose which cookie categories to enable

### 5.2 Manage Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:

- Visit **Account Settings → Privacy & Cookies**
- Update your consent choices
- Your new preferences apply immediately

### 5.3 Browser-Based Cookie Control

Your browser may allow you to:

- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete existing cookies
- View stored cookies

**Note**: Blocking cookies may prevent the platform from functioning correctly.

### 5.4 Opt-Out of Specific Advertisers

You may opt-out of advertising cookies from specific providers:

- **Google**: [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com)
- **Facebook**: [Facebook Ad Preferences](https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences)
- **LinkedIn**: [LinkedIn Ad Preferences](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/62931)
- **IAB**: [Your Online Choices](http://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)

## 6. Cookie Retention and Deletion

| Cookie Type | Retention Duration | Deletion on Account Closure |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Duration of session + preferences | Preferences retained 1 year |
| Analytics | 12 months | Deleted after 90 days |
| Advertising | 6 months | Deleted after 90 days |

**Note**: Audit records of your cookie consent choices are retained for 5 years for legal compliance, but cannot be used for tracking or advertising.

## 8. GDPR and Privacy Compliance

### 8.1 Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request all data held about you
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request data deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Restriction**: Limit how your data is processed
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection**: Object to specific processing activities

### 8.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact:

- **Email**: privacy@trade-tender.co.uk
- **Data Protection Officer**: dpo@trade-tender.co.uk
- **Written Request**: Trade Tender, Data Protection Officer, [Company Address]

Response time: 30 days

## 9. Adspace-Specific Cookie Terms

**When Adspace is NOT Active**:
- Advertising cookies are NOT deployed
- Advertising-related tracking is disabled
- No third-party advertising data is shared

**When Adspace IS Active**:
- Advertising cookies are deployed only with your consent
- You must explicitly accept advertising cookies to see targeted ads
- You can withdraw consent at any time (ads will no longer be targeted)
- Existing consents are honored across all Trade Tender platforms
