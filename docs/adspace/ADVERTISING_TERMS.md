# Trade Tender Advertising Terms

These Advertising Terms apply when Trade Tender's advertising space (adspace) feature is activated by Trade Tender administrators. These terms govern the use of advertising on the Trade Tender platform and apply to all users, advertisers, and related parties.

**Effective when**: ADSPACE_ACTIVE platform setting is enabled.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Advertiser"**: Any third party who purchases advertising space or advertising services on the platform
- **"Advertising Content"**: Any promotional material, banner, video, text, or interactive element provided by an Advertiser
- **"Cookie"**: A data file stored on a user's device for tracking, personalization, or functionality purposes
- **"Essential Cookies"**: Cookies required for basic platform functionality (may be used without consent)
- **"Non-Essential Cookies"**: Analytics, advertising, and preference cookies (requires user consent)
- **"Trade Tender"**: The platform operator and service provider
- **"User"**: Any person accessing Trade Tender

## 3. Advertising Presence

### 3.1 Disclosure

All advertising on Trade Tender will be:

- Clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Advertisement"
- Visually distinguished from organic platform content
- Accompanied by advertiser identification
- Removable via user controls (where technically feasible)

### 3.2 Placement and Display

Advertising may be displayed in:

- Dedicated advertising regions (banners, sidebar spaces)
- Search results and filtered results pages
- Email communications (with separate opt-in)
- Mobile app interfaces

Advertising will NOT interfere with:

- Core tender matching algorithms

### 3.3 Frequency and Limits

Trade Tender may implement frequency caps and display limits to prevent:

- Excessive advertising exposure

## 4. Cookie and Tracking Terms

### 4.1 Cookie Types and Usage

**Essential Cookies** (No Consent Required):
- Session management
- Authentication and security
- Platform functionality
- Language preferences

**Non-Essential Cookies** (Explicit Consent Required):
- Analytics and performance tracking
- Advertising and retargeting
- User preference personalization
- Behavioral analysis

### 4.2 User Consent

By continuing to use Trade Tender after adspace is activated:

1. You will be presented with a cookie consent banner on first visit
2. You may:
   - Accept all cookies (including advertising)
   - Accept only essential cookies (no advertising cookies)
   - Manage individual cookie categories
   - Withdraw consent at any time

3. Your consent choices are:
   - Recorded and stored
   - Respected across your sessions
   - Honored across Trade Tender's domains
   - Retained for 2 years

### 4.3 Third-Party Cookies

Trade Tender may share non-personal data with Advertisers through:

- Aggregate conversion tracking
- Non-identifying audience insights
- Campaign performance metrics

Third parties are contractually bound to respect user privacy and comply with GDPR and UK regulations.

### 4.4 Cookie Management

You may:

1. **Accept Cookies**: Select "Accept All" on the cookie banner
2. **Decline Non-Essential**: Select "Essential Only" to block advertising cookies
3. **Customize**: Choose specific cookie categories to enable
4. **Withdraw Consent**: Update your preferences at any time in account settings
5. **Browser Controls**: Use browser settings to block or limit cookies (may affect functionality)

## 5. Privacy and Data Protection

### 5.1 Data Sharing

Trade Tender will NOT share with Advertisers:

- Your email address or contact details
- Your full name (unless you have explicitly authorized)

- Your personal browsing history outside Trade Tender

### 5.2 GDPR Compliance

All advertising and cookie practices comply with:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

- ePrivacy Directives

### 5.3 Data Retention

Advertising-related data is retained for:

- **User consent records**: 2 years after consent withdrawal or account deletion

- **Advertiser audit trails**: 5 years (for compliance purposes)
