# Advertising Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines establish standards for all advertising content displayed on Trade Tender. Compliance ensures the platform maintains its neutral position and protects users from inappropriate, misleading, or harmful advertising.

## Prohibited Advertising Content

The following advertising types are strictly prohibited:

### Content-Based Restrictions

- **Misinformation**: Any advertising containing false, misleading, or deceptive claims
- **Illegal Activities**: Advertising for illegal products or services
- **Harassment or Hate**: Content promoting discrimination, harassment, or hate based on protected characteristics
- **Violence or Exploitation**: Advertising depicting violence, child exploitation, or human trafficking
- **Unproven Medical/Health Claims**: Medical products without verified clinical evidence
- **Financial Fraud**: Unregulated financial services, cryptocurrency scams, or investment fraud
- **Weapons or Explosives**: Advertising for weapons beyond licensed/regulated contexts

### Platform-Competitive Restrictions

- **Direct Competition**: Advertising directly competing with Trade Tender's core business model
- **Tender Interference**: Any advertising attempting to bypass the tender matching system or influence Client decisions outside the proper workflow
- **Recruitment Conflicts**: During active tenders, advertising from Retailers competing in those same tenders

### Privacy and Data Restrictions

- **User Data Abuse**: Advertising that requires or implies collection of user data outside approved mechanisms
- **Tracking Concerns**: Advertising using excessive or deceptive tracking that violates user consent

## Advertising Categories - Allowed with Approval

The following categories may be advertised with prior approval and compliance verification:

- **Construction Industry Services**: Tools, materials, equipment, training (verified and approved)
- **Business Services**: Accounting, legal, insurance services relevant to construction businesses
- **Professional Development**: Industry certifications and training programs
- **Trade Publications**: Industry magazines, journals, and news services
- **Technology Solutions**: Software and tools relevant to the construction industry

## Audit and Review Process

### Pre-Deployment

1. **Submission**: Advertisers submit content for review at least 14 days before deployment
2. **Initial Review**: Compliance team reviews against these guidelines
3. **Risk Assessment**: Evaluation for potential platform conflicts or user impact
4. **Approval**: Final approval by designated Super User (if activated)

### Post-Deployment

1. **Monitoring**: Regular monitoring for policy violations
2. **User Reports**: Mechanisms for users to report inappropriate advertising
3. **Removal**: Non-compliant content removed within 24 hours of detection
4. **Notification**: Advertiser notified of removal and reason

### Repeat Violations

- **First Violation**: Written warning and content review process
- **Second Violation**: 30-day suspension from platform advertising
- **Third Violation**: Permanent suspension from platform advertising

## User Protection Measures

### Transparency

- All advertising is clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Advertisement"
- Advertiser identity is disclosed
- Clear distinction between advertising and organic platform content

### User Controls

- Users can report inappropriate advertising
- Users can opt-out of certain advertising categories (where technically feasible)
- Cookie preferences control tracking behavior

### Accessibility

- All advertising content must meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards
- Alt text for all images
- Closed captions for video content

## Cookie and Tracking Standards

### Permitted Tracking

- **Essential Cookies**: For platform functionality (no consent required)
- **Analytics Cookies**: Aggregated, anonymized usage data (with user consent)
- **Advertising Cookies**: Third-party tracking for ad targeting (explicit consent required)

### Prohibited Tracking

- **Cross-device Tracking**: Without explicit user consent
- **Behavioral Targeting Conflicts**: That create platform conflicts of interest
- **Covert Tracking**: Hidden trackers or deceptive tracking mechanisms

## Geographic and Regulatory Compliance

### UK and EU Regulations

- **GDPR Compliance**: All advertising respects GDPR principles
- **UK PECR**: Electronic marketing regulations observed
- **Consumer Protection**: UK Consumer Rights Act compliance

### Content Localization

- All advertising must be relevant to UK market
- Language: English (UK spellings and conventions)
- Currency: GBP (British Pounds)

## Seasonal and Event-Based Restrictions

- **Tender Period Conflicts**: No advertising from tender participants during active tender evaluation periods
- **Critical Events**: Advertising may be restricted during platform-critical events or maintenance

## Escalation and Appeals

### Content Rejection

Advertisers have the right to appeal rejected content within 7 days by providing:

1. Detailed written explanation of compliance
2. Evidence addressing specific guideline concerns
3. Revised content (if applicable)

### Appeals Process

1. **Submission**: Appeal submitted to compliance@tradetender.co.uk
2. **Review**: Independent review by designated Super User
3. **Decision**: Final decision within 7 days
4. **Notification**: Outcome communicated to advertiser

## Compliance Certification

All advertisers must:

1. Certify compliance with these guidelines before deployment
2. Acknowledge understanding of audit and removal processes
3. Agree to indemnify Trade Tender from guideline violations
4. Provide valid contact information and dispute resolution details

## Reporting and Documentation

The platform maintains records of:

- All submitted advertising content
- Compliance review decisions and reasoning
- User reports and complaints
- Content violations and removals
- Audit trail for Super User approvals

Records are retained for 5 years in compliance with audit requirements.

## Changes to Guidelines

These guidelines may be updated at any time by Trade Tender. Advertisers will be notified of material changes, and continuing participation implies acceptance of updated guidelines.
